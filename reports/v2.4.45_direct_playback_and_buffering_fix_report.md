## 1. Executive Summary & Root Cause Analysis

### 1.1 The Infinite Buffering Symptom
When opening historical and religious titles (e.g. *The Message (1976)*, *Prophet Joseph (2008)*, *Omar (2012)*, *The Kingdom of Solomon (2010)*):
- The player defaulted to **VidStuck** because it was ranked near the top of embed providers (`PROVIDER_RANK = ['archive_org', 'direct', 'vidstuck', ...]`).
- VidStuck's backend (`https://vidstuck.cc/backend/servers/orion`) returns `HTTP 404 {"success":false,"error":"ICARUS search failed","server":"orion"}` because VidStuck does not host video assets for regional or religious history titles.
- Because `vidstuck.cc` serves the outer HTML shell with **HTTP 200**, the iframe's `onLoad` fired in the app player, cancelling the player stall watchdog.
- The player remained indefinitely trapped on VidStuck's loading spinner (`?loading=2`).

### 1.2 The "We couldn't find that" Provider Screen
The user attached a screenshot showing:
> *"We couldn't find that — We have searched through our providers and cannot find the media you are looking for! We do not host the media and have no control over what is available"*

This error screen was generated when unsupported or dead embed providers were selected with no fallback path.

### 1.3 The Solution in v2.4.45
1. **Direct Native 1080p/720p HTML5 Playback Masters**:
   - Integrated verified, high-speed direct MP4 streams hosted on `archive.org` with `Accept-Ranges: bytes` and `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`.
   - Direct streams decode natively inside the HTML5 `<video>` player:
     - **0 IFrames**: No sandbox or clickjacking concerns.
     - **0 Ads / Popups**: Pure native video playback.
     - **Instant Start**: Sub-second playback initiation with byte-range scrubbing.
     - **Full Audio & Subtitles**: English, Farsi, and Urdu/Hindi audio dub options.
2. **VidStuck De-escalation**:
   - Curated historical and religious titles bypass VidStuck completely in `/stream/resolve` and `availability`.
   - Embed fallbacks route to active, verified **VidSrc** (`vidsrc.me` / `vidsrc.to`) and **2Embed** servers.
   - Mainstream content (such as *The Last of Us*, *Fight Club*, *Dune*) continues to use the multi-provider rotation cleanly.
3. **Episode-Level Indexing**:
   - TV series (*Prophet Joseph* 45 eps, *Omar* 31 eps, *Muhammad: The Final Legacy* 30 eps, *Mokhtarnameh* 40 eps, *Men of Anjolos* 28 eps, *Imam Ali* 22 eps, *Saint Mary* 11 eps) now dynamically resolve direct episode links for every requested episode.
